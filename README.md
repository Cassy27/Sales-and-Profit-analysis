This project analyzes retail sales and profitability performance using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis focuses on understanding the relationship between sales and profit, identifying underperforming products and categories, and uncovering opportunities to improve overall business profitability.
The business generated approximately $2.30M in total sales, $286.40K in total profit, with an overall 12.5% profit margin, across approximately 5K orders and 793 customers.
Although the business experienced strong sales performance and consistent profit growth from 2014 to 2017, deeper analysis revealed significant differences in profitability across product categories and customer segments.
Technology generated the highest sales and profit, while Furniture generated relatively strong sales but significantly lower profit. Further analysis of Furniture identified Tables, Bookcases and Supplies as major areas contributing to losses, with Tables recording the largest loss of approximately $18K.
The analysis also showed that the Consumer segment generated the highest sales, while Home Office achieved the highest profit margin.
The findings were translated into recommendations focused on improving profitability, optimizing product and discount strategies, sustaining Technology's performance, and creating more balanced growth across the business.
